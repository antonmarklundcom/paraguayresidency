Homepage design preview. A dossier, not a brochure: the catch is stated above the pitch. Desktop 1440 above, mobile 390 below.

Mobile — 390 × 844 (scrolls)

Designer’s note. Cool and institutional on purpose: ground #EDF1EF, surface white, ink #131A18, hairlines #D2DAD6, one accent #2F6B57 used only for the primary CTA, the section numerals and the verified marker. It should look like it was made by people who are not trying to excite you. Type: Space Grotesk display (technical, slightly odd, not corporate), Inter body, IBM Plex Mono labels.

The idea to judge: “What the catch is” is the section immediately after the hero — before the routes, before the tax explainer, before any selling. Every competitor buries this in an FAQ. Putting it at position one is the brand, and the dark inversion makes it the visual centre of the page as well as the argument.

CRO: the catch block is the highest-converting thing on a page like this — a sceptical reader who sees their objection stated first stops looking for the trick; the route table gives each row its own entry point; “presence” is a column in that table because it is the real buying question; the form asks what you are weighing rather than demanding a phone number; sticky bottom bar on mobile carries one action only.
