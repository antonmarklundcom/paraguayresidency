Paraguay Residency Guide — Direction A, “The Record”

Homepage design preview. The page is the artefact it sells: a letterpress contents page you can buy. Desktop 1440 above, mobile 390 below.

Desktop — 1440 × auto
Mobile — 390 × 844 (scrolls)

Designer’s note. Paper ground #F7F1E6, ink #1A1613, hairlines #DDD1BC, one accent — letterpress oxblood #9A3B2C — on the buy button, the chapter numerals and the verified marker only. Type: Newsreader display (a text face, set large, so it reads like a printed book rather than a landing page), Inter body, IBM Plex Mono labels.

The idea to judge: the twelve chapters are not a feature list, they are a real contents page — numerals hanging in the margin, rules between entries, a leader line to what each chapter settles. It is the product, shown rather than described, and it is the section that has to sell the thing.

CRO: price is in the nav from the first pixel and never leaves the screen (sticky bar on mobile); risk reversal (14-day refund, 12 months of updates, instant delivery) sits inside the price block, not in the footer; one primary action on the page — “Get the guide” — with the email capture as the only secondary, placed after the price so it catches the people who already said no; the “what this is not” section deliberately disqualifies the wrong buyer before the refund does it for them.

Price renders as the literal {{PRICE}} token — it is fetched from the products table at runtime, so no number is hardcoded anywhere.
