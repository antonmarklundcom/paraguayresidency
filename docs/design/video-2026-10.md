Anton asked on 2026-09-28 for Seedance 2.5 videos and for advice on 720p vs 1080p and on hosting (Gumlet,
Cloudflare, Cloudinary).

## What's shipped

accepted hero still, no audio). Each clip was drafted at 480p (18 credits) and reviewed frame by frame, then
finalized at 1080p (72 credits). Six of them use the same still as start and end frame, so they loop seamlessly.
The hub clip (a man walking down the colonnade) plays once and holds its last frame.

## How it loads (`src/components/HeroVideo.tsx`)

The hero **photo stays the LCP element**. The `<video>` element is not rendered until the page has fired `load`
and the browser is idle, and then only when the viewport is ≥ 1024 px, the user hasn't asked for reduced motion
and data saver is off. It picks the 1080 file when `innerWidth × devicePixelRatio > 1700`, and the 720 file
otherwise. It fades in over the photo once it's actually playing, so a slow network just keeps showing the
still. Phones never download a byte of video. Lighthouse mobile is unaffected by design.

## 720p or 1080p?

**1080p, for these.** They're full-bleed desktop backgrounds; 720p stretched across a 1440–1920 px screen looks
soft next to crisp type. The cost is small anyway: a calm 6-second loop is about 1–2.5 MB at 1080p and well under
1 MB at 720p. We ship both and let the component choose. For anything shown in a smaller frame (a card, a
play-on-click explainer), 720p is enough.

## Hosting: what to use when

- **Short muted loops (these): self-host, as now.** They're static files under 3 MB each; serving them from the
  app (`public/videos/`) is simplest and has no vendor lock-in or per-view cost.
- **Put Cloudflare (free plan) in front of all seven domains.** This is the single biggest media win. It gives a
  global CDN cache for images, fonts and these videos, Brotli, HTTP/3, and a reliable "purge everything"
  button. It also replaces the Hostinger CDN, which today caches HTML for a year and broke the Spanish homepage
  (see `docs/audit/2026-10/seo-gap.md` T2). Moving nameservers is Anton's action; plan it together with fixing
  the four domains that don't resolve.
- **Gumlet for long-form video with sound** (Anton to camera, client video testimonials, a 30–60 s "what happens
  after you message us" explainer). You get adaptive HLS streaming, automatic 240p→1080p renditions, poster
  frames, captions and view analytics, and you already have access. Embed with a click-to-play facade, so
  nothing loads until the visitor presses play.
- **Cloudinary: not needed.** webimg already produces AVIF/WebP at the right sizes at build time, and Gumlet
  covers streaming. Cloudinary would duplicate both at a higher price.
