The Investor Pass is often described as if it were a temporary status that you later have to turn into something permanent. It is not. What the Pass produces, per the resolution: <Fact k="investorpass.cie_scope" />. So the residency you end up with is the permanent kind, and there is no second conversion step waiting at the end.

## What actually has a date on it

Validity of the residency: <Fact k="investorpass.validity_years" />.

So the thing that comes round is the renewal of the resident card, not a change of status. Treat the card date the way you would treat a passport date: put it in a calendar a year ahead, and start the paperwork well before it, not the week you notice it.

## What to keep in order

- **Your file from the original approval.** Keep copies of the investor certificate, the residency resolution and the receipts for the investment. If anything is asked later, you want them in one place.
- **Recency-limited documents.** A renewal can ask for documents with a validity window, such as a police record. These do not update themselves, and starting late on them is the most common avoidable delay.
- **Your presence history.** Permanent residency comes with its own presence expectations. Confirm how your real travel pattern fits them, rather than assuming.
- **A plan before you touch the investment.** Whether you may sell, move or restructure the qualifying investment without affecting your standing is a question about your case. Ask us in writing first.

## Family members on the same case

If your family was added to the original case, each person's card and documents still need their own attention at renewal time. Recency-limited documents do not update just because the household renews together. See [bringing your family](/insights/bringing-your-family) for how dependents were handled the first time.

## The honest summary

There is no expiry date to fear, but a card that lapses is still a real problem, and the rules on the investment after approval are worth confirming before you act. If you are unsure, bring the question to us before it becomes urgent. If your longer-term goal is a Paraguayan passport rather than residency, read [Paraguay citizenship by investment](/insights/paraguay-citizenship-by-investment) first: the route runs through years of residence, not through the investment.
