"Paraguay golden visa" is what investors type into a search box. The Paraguayan government doesn't use the phrase. The program it describes is the Paraguay Investor Pass, and the legal document behind it is the Constancia de Inversionista Extranjero (CIE), a foreign investor certificate that opens a direct route to permanent residency. This piece sets out what the program is, the four routes, the real cost, the documents, the timeline and the things it does not do.

## What the "golden visa" actually is

Strip away the marketing and the Investor Pass is two steps. First, <Fact k="investorpass.cie_scope" />. Second, the migration office (Dirección Nacional de Migraciones, DNM) grants permanent residency on the strength of that certificate, without the temporary stage everyone else goes through first.

The legal instrument is <Fact k="investorpass.resolution_history" />. The program was <Fact k="investorpass.launch_date" />. That history matters in practice: web pages written under the earlier resolutions describe a single productive-investment route with job creation. The current text has four.

## The four routes and their minimums

Each route is a separate category with its own minimum and its own evidence. You qualify under one of them; they are not added together.

| Route | Minimum and core condition | What the file has to show |
|---|---|---|
| Productive business | <Fact k="investorpass.route_business_usd" /> | A business plan in industry, commerce or services, an execution schedule and progress reports |
| Tourism | <Fact k="investorpass.route_tourism_usd" /> | A tourism business plan, an execution schedule and progress reports |
| Real estate | <Fact k="investorpass.route_real_estate_usd" /> | A registered deed or a notarised purchase contract |
| Financial instruments | <Fact k="investorpass.route_financial_usd" /> | A certificate from an authorised Paraguayan securities entity |

- **Financial instruments:** <Fact k="investorpass.financial_conditions" />. The route and its tourism twin are covered in [the financial instruments and tourism routes, in detail](/insights/financial-instruments-and-tourism-routes).

## What it costs in total

The minimum investment is not a fee. The capital stays yours, in a building, a business, a project or a portfolio. The real costs of the program sit around it:

- **Government fees**, which in Paraguay are <Fact k="fees.basis" />.
- **Documents**: apostilles and certificates in your home country, Spanish translations, and the Paraguayan Interpol certificate at <Fact k="fees.interpol_certificate" />.
- **Transaction costs** on the investment itself, such as notary fees on a property purchase or setup costs for a company.
- **Professional fees** for structuring and filing. Ours are quoted in writing before anything is filed, together with the timeline and the exit options.
- **The cost of holding the asset**: management, upkeep and the return you give up by committing capital to it.

Ask anyone quoting you a "golden visa price" which of those lines it includes.

## The documents

On the personal side, the resolution asks for <Fact k="investorpass.cie_documents" />. Foreign documents have to be <Fact k="investorpass.foreign_documents" />.

The route-specific evidence comes on top: a business plan with solvency evidence for the productive and tourism routes, the deed or contract for real estate, the securities certificate for financial instruments. Our [requirements page](/investor-pass/requirements) keeps the full list.

## Process and timeline

1. **Choose and structure the route.** Match the route to your capital, liquidity needs and appetite for running something.
2. **Make or commit the investment**, documented in the way the resolution asks.
3. **File for the CIE with SUACE.** The certificate is issued <Fact k="investorpass.cie_issuing_term" />.
4. **File for permanent residency with DNM** using the certificate.
5. **Collect your cédula**, the Paraguayan identity card: <Fact k="cedula.timeline" />.

## What you get

Permanent residency: <Fact k="investorpass.validity_years" />. It comes with one ongoing condition that catches investors who never intend to live here: <Fact k="permanent.presence_rule" />. The route-specific reporting obligations described above continue after approval, and SUACE monitors them.

Family members are not covered by the resolution itself, which issues the certificate only to the investor as an individual. Spouses and minor children are usually handled through the migration office alongside the investor's residency; our [family guide](/insights/bringing-your-family) explains how.

## What it is not

- **Not citizenship.** Paraguay has no citizenship-by-investment program. Naturalization comes later, on residence and other conditions. See [Paraguay citizenship by investment](/insights/paraguay-citizenship-by-investment).
- **Not a European visa.** Paraguayan residency gives you no right to live anywhere else.
- **Not a tax exemption.** Paraguay taxes territorially, <Fact k="tax.territorial_rate" />, and your home country's rules still apply. Our [tax piece](/insights/investor-pass-and-your-taxes) sets out the questions for your adviser.
- **Not guaranteed.** SUACE can reject an incomplete or non-compliant file, and anti-money-laundering review applies to the source of funds.

## How it compares

Against the standard route, the Investor Pass buys time, not a different outcome: the permanent card is the same. Our [comparison with standard residency](/investor-pass/vs-standard-residency) covers when that time is worth the capital. Against other countries' golden visas, Paraguay's minimums are modest, the program is young and the rules were rewritten in 2026, which is why we quote from the resolution rather than from summaries. See [the Investor Pass against Uruguay and Panama](/insights/investor-pass-vs-uruguay-panama). And if you have read about a separate "SUACE residency", our piece on [the Investor Pass and SUACE](/insights/investor-pass-vs-suace) explains why they are the same channel.

## Is it right for you?

The Investor Pass fits investors who have a real reason to put capital into Paraguay, can document where the money comes from, and value skipping the temporary stage. It fits less well if the investment would exist only to obtain the residency, if you need the capital liquid, or if you are really looking for a second passport.
