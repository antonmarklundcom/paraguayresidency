The Investor Pass is a route to permanent residency for people who bring
qualifying capital into Paraguay. Programme launch: <Fact k="investorpass.launch_date" />.

## What it changes

The standard path is temporary residency first, permanent later. The Pass
removes that first stage for applicants who qualify.

That is the central proposition: a different basis for applying for permanent
residency. It is not an approval purchased at a fixed price. The applicant,
the investment and the supporting evidence still need to satisfy the current
requirements. Removing a stage from the immigration route does not remove
the work needed to establish eligibility.

For an investor already considering Paraguay, that distinction can matter.
For someone who only wants a residence option and has no reason to deploy
capital locally, it may not. The right comparison is the ordinary residency
route against an investment route assessed on its full obligations, rather
than against a promise of speed.

## What is clear, and what needs confirming

The route's purpose is direct permanent residency through qualifying
investment. The practical terms of your application need a closer review:
which investment qualifies, how it must be held, what evidence is accepted
and what obligations follow approval. Treat these as questions to resolve
before a transaction, not as paperwork to tidy up afterwards.

Our shared figures remain unverified where the underlying requirements have
not been confirmed. That is why the wording here is qualified. An advertised
minimum, a developer's brochure and a description of a successful case are
not substitutes for checking the applicable resolution and current procedure.

Ask for a written explanation that connects your proposed investment to the
route being recommended. It should identify what has been confirmed and
what still depends on review. If the answer is simply that other clients
have done something similar, the central eligibility question remains open.

## The threshold

Public sources disagree with each other on the minimum. What we will tell you is
that it starts <Fact k="investorpass.min_investment_usd" />, and that we confirm
the current figure against the resolution before you commit anything.

The minimum also needs a definition. Ask what counts towards it, what proof
is required and whether transaction costs sit outside the qualifying amount.
The value of an asset, the amount transferred and the amount recognised for
immigration purposes should not be assumed to be interchangeable.

Keep investment capital separate from professional fees, official charges,
document preparation and travel in your budget. Capital committed to an
asset has an opportunity cost even when it remains yours. Whether you can
recover it, on what terms and with what consequences for residency are
questions to settle before relying on an exit plan.

## The investment routes, in practical terms

The routes discussed on this site concern real estate, productive business,
financial instruments and tourism projects. They call for different
commercial judgments as well as confirmation of immigration eligibility.
The following descriptions are starting points for that review, not a list
of preapproved assets.

- **Real estate:** <Fact k="investorpass.route_real_estate_usd" />. Investigate
  title, the seller, contractual protections and the property itself. A
  property being marketed to foreign buyers does not establish that it
  qualifies for the residence route.
- **Productive business:** <Fact k="investorpass.route_business_usd" />. The
  business plan, ownership structure and evidence of capital deployment need
  to fit together. Consider who will run the business and how you will
  supervise it if you live elsewhere.
- **Financial instruments:** <Fact k="investorpass.route_financial_usd" />.
  Understand the issuer, custody arrangements, access to your capital and
  the terms of the instrument before comparing it with a property purchase.
- **Tourism:** <Fact k="investorpass.route_tourism_usd" />. Confirm the project's
  eligibility separately from its commercial appeal, and examine the
  operator, permissions and contractual obligations before committing funds.

The real estate deep dive in the related reading below develops the property
questions further. Whichever route you examine, immigration qualification
and investment quality need their own conclusions. An eligible investment
can still be unsuitable for your risk tolerance or liquidity needs.

## What the application involves

Start with your nationality, residence history, intended family applicants
and the proposed source of capital. Those details determine what needs to
be checked. A transaction involving personal savings calls for a different
document discussion from capital arriving through a company or asset sale.
Describe the actual structure before anyone prepares a filing plan.

Next, coordinate personal documents and investment evidence. Ask which
foreign records need authentication or translation, who checks them and how
their preparation fits the investment sequence. Obtain advice on the order
of commitments and on contractual protections if eligibility or the
application does not proceed as expected.

For timing, use <Fact k="investorpass.timeline" />. Preparation, submission
and approval are different milestones. The timeline article in the related
reading explains why a filing estimate cannot describe the entire project.
Have the professional managing your case distinguish what you control from
what depends on a counterparty or the reviewing authority.

## Validity

Residency card validity: <Fact k="investorpass.validity_years" />.

Permanent residency also carries an ongoing obligation:
<Fact k="permanent.presence_rule" />. Discuss your travel pattern and ask how
card renewal, continued eligibility and any investment conditions apply to
your situation. The word permanent should not be read as permission to stop
tracking the conditions attached to your status.

The identity document is a separate practical milestone:
<Fact k="cedula.timeline" />. Family eligibility, banking arrangements and tax
residence also need their own assessment. Do not infer an answer to any of
those questions from the fact that the principal applicant can pursue the
investment route.

## Who it suits

The Pass is worth examining if you have a considered reason to invest in
Paraguay, can document the funds and value direct access to the permanent
application route. It deserves more caution if you would choose the asset
only to obtain the status, need quick access to the capital or are depending
on a guaranteed completion date.

A useful first consultation ends with a proposed route, a list of unresolved
questions and a clear account of the work required before commitment. You
should understand why that route fits your circumstances and what would
make the team advise against it. That is a firmer basis for a decision than
the lowest threshold quoted online.
