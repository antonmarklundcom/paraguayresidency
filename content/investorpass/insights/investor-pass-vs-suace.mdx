Search for "residencia permanente Paraguay SUACE" and you find pages describing a SUACE residency with a single minimum and a jobs requirement. Search for "Paraguay Investor Pass" and you find four routes and different minimums. Investors reasonably ask whether these are two programs and which one to pick. They are one channel, described at two points in time. This piece explains what SUACE is, how its investor route became the Investor Pass, and the comparison that actually matters: investor residency against the standard route.

## What SUACE is

SUACE, the Sistema Unificado de Apertura y Cierre de Empresas, is the Ministry of Industry and Commerce's one-stop office for opening and closing companies, created by law in 2013. Inside it, the Directorate of Investments and Special Regimes reviews investor applications and evaluates business plans, and a verification directorate follows up on them afterwards.

What SUACE issues for residency purposes is <Fact k="investorpass.cie_scope" />. The migration office then grants the residency itself. So SUACE is the door to investor residency, not a separate residency.

## How the SUACE route became the Investor Pass

The investor certificate has been regulated several times. The current text is <Fact k="investorpass.resolution_history" />. The Investor Pass name arrived with the current rules: the program was <Fact k="investorpass.launch_date" />.

Under the earlier resolutions, the certificate was essentially one route: a productive investment that created jobs. That route is still there, unchanged in its headline minimum: <Fact k="investorpass.route_business_usd" />. What the 2026 resolution did was add three passive routes beside it, each with its own minimum and its own evidence.

| | Before the 2026 resolution | Investor Pass today |
|---|---|---|
| Routes | One productive route | Productive business, tourism, real estate, financial instruments |
| Jobs required | Yes | Only on the productive route |
| Business plan | Yes | Productive and tourism routes |
| Issued by | SUACE | SUACE |
| Residency granted by | Migration office (DNM) | Migration office (DNM) |

Applications already pending under the previous resolution were moved onto the new rules where those are more favorable to the applicant. If you have an older quote or checklist that mentions a SUACE residency, it is not wrong about the channel, only out of date about the routes.

## Why the old phrase survives

The migration office's own pages, agency websites and forum threads still say "SUACE residency", and in Spanish "residencia permanente para inversionistas SUACE". That is why the phrase keeps appearing in searches. When a provider uses it, ask one question: which route under Resolution 0283/2026 are you proposing, and what evidence does that route need? If they can't answer, their information predates the current rules.

## How a file moves through SUACE today

Whatever name a provider uses, an investor file now follows the same sequence:

1. **Choose the route and the structure.** Productive business, tourism, real estate or financial instruments, held personally or through a company you can show you own.
2. **Make or commit the investment.** <Fact k="investorpass.investment_status" />.
3. **Complete the online application at the Ministry of Industry and Commerce.** The form counts as a sworn statement, so the amount and any jobs you declare have legal weight. It goes in with your personal documents and the route-specific evidence: a business plan for the productive and tourism routes, a deed or notarized contract for real estate, a securities certificate for financial instruments.
4. **SUACE reviews the file.** Its investments directorate checks the documents and, where there is one, the business plan, then issues the certificate <Fact k="investorpass.cie_issuing_term" />. Anti-money-laundering review of the source of funds runs alongside.
5. **Apply to the migration office** for permanent residency, with the certificate.
6. **Collect the cédula**, Paraguay's identity card: <Fact k="cedula.timeline" />.
7. **Keep reporting.** SUACE follows up after issue, checking projects against their plans and reporting to the migration office.

The only step that belongs to SUACE alone is the fourth. Everything after it is ordinary permanent residency, handled the same way as on the standard route.

## The comparison that matters: investor route vs standard route

The real choice for most investors is not SUACE versus the Investor Pass. It is investor residency against the standard, temporary-first route that anyone with documented means can use.

| | Investor Pass (through SUACE) | Standard route |
|---|---|---|
| First status | Permanent residency, directly | Temporary residency: <Fact k="temporary.duration" /> |
| What you must show | A qualifying investment, from <Fact k="investorpass.min_investment_usd" /> | Documented means: <Fact k="solvency.requirement" /> |
| Who decides | SUACE (certificate), then the migration office | The migration office |
| Capital tied up | Yes, on the chosen route's terms | No |
| Obligations after approval | Route-specific reporting to SUACE, plus the presence rule | The presence rule |
| Time to permanent residency | <Fact k="investorpass.timeline" /> | The temporary period first, then a change of category |

Both routes end in the same permanent residency, with the same condition: <Fact k="permanent.presence_rule" />. Our [comparison with standard residency](/investor-pass/vs-standard-residency) puts it simply: the Pass buys time, not a different outcome.

## When the investor route is worth it

- You already intend to invest in Paraguay, so the capital is not committed purely to obtain residency.
- You want permanent status now, without the temporary stage and its conversion step.
- You can document the source of funds cleanly, because anti-money-laundering review is part of the certificate.
- For the financial route in particular, you are comfortable with <Fact k="investorpass.financial_conditions" />.

## When the standard route is the better buy

- You have no other reason to invest here, and the capital would earn more elsewhere.
- You can show documented means under the standard rules and don't mind the temporary period.
- You need liquidity, or you want to keep your options open about how much time you will actually spend in Paraguay.

## If you choose the investor route

Pick the route before you pick the asset. Each route has its own evidence, from a registered deed to a securities certificate, and a purchase that doesn't match a route's conditions doesn't qualify however large it is.
