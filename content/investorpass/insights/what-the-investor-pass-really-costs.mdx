People compare residency-by-investment programmes on the headline number. That is a mistake, because two programmes with the same headline can differ by tens of thousands in fees, holding costs and time. This page lists what to count for the Paraguay Investor Pass, in the order the money leaves your account.

## 1. The investment itself

The minimum depends on the route: <Fact k="investorpass.min_investment_usd" />. The route thresholds are on their own pages: <Fact k="investorpass.route_real_estate_usd" />; <Fact k="investorpass.route_business_usd" />; <Fact k="investorpass.route_financial_usd" />; <Fact k="investorpass.route_tourism_usd" />. Compare them in the [golden visa overview](/insights/paraguay-golden-visa).

## 2. Residency fees

Once you hold the investor certificate, residency follows. The permanent residency fee is <Fact k="fees.permanent_residency" />. Those fees are set in minimum daily wages: <Fact k="fees.basis" />. The card is renewed later: <Fact k="fees.permanent_card_renewal" />.

## 3. Documents

Apostilles, sworn translations and certificates come from several authorities in your home country and in Paraguay. There is no honest single number, because it depends on your nationality and the number of people in the file. Use the [document checklist](/insights/investor-pass-document-checklist) to count them.

## 4. Professional fees

Legal, notarial and advisory costs vary with the route. Real estate involves a notary; a business involves company formation and accounting. Our own fee is confirmed in writing (<Fact k="pricing.investor_pass" />), and we tell you what falls outside it.

## 5. Holding or running costs

| Route | After you file |
|---|---|
| Real estate | Property tax, maintenance, any agent |
| Productive business | Payroll, reporting, running costs |
| Financial instruments | Custody and product costs |
| Tourism | Development and operating costs |

## 6. Time

Time is money in this programme. The rough sequence is <Fact k="investorpass.timeline" />, and delays cost in extra rent and travel. See [how long it actually takes](/insights/how-long-it-actually-takes).

## Tax is separate

None of this covers tax. For that, read [the Investor Pass and your taxes](/insights/investor-pass-and-your-taxes), and take advice in your home country.
