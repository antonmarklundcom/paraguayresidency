Every route into the Investor Pass has a catch. For real estate it is the paperwork on the property. For financial instruments it is the holding period. For the productive route, the catch is that you are running a business. This page explains what that means so you can decide whether the lower entry figure is worth it.

## The figure and what it buys

The threshold is <Fact k="investorpass.route_business_usd" />, and across routes the range is <Fact k="investorpass.min_investment_usd" />. This route is the direct descendant of the older SUACE route: <Fact k="suace.status" />. For how the two names relate, read [Investor Pass vs SUACE](/insights/investor-pass-vs-suace).

## What the plan has to show

<Fact k="investorpass.productive_conditions" />.

Three parts deserve attention:

1. **The activity.** Industry, commerce or services. It has to be a real operation, not a holding company with nothing under it.
2. **The jobs.** Formal, registered local jobs, not contractors abroad.
3. **The reports.** Progress reports every six months are part of the deal. Build a bookkeeping habit before you file.

## What does not count

Rent, salaries, utilities and recurring administrative costs are excluded from the investment figure. A plan that spends the required amount mostly on running costs will not meet the threshold. The money has to go into the productive base: equipment, premises, stock or capital, as the plan sets out.

## Who it suits

- Operators who already run or plan to run a business in Paraguay.
- Investors comfortable with local hiring and reporting.

## Who it does not suit

- Anyone wanting a passive holding. See the [real estate route](/insights/real-estate-route-deep-dive) or the [financial and tourism routes](/insights/financial-instruments-and-tourism-routes).
- Someone who cannot commit to running the business through the reporting period.

## Practical order

1. Draft the business plan first, with an honest headcount.
2. Check that the spend excludes running costs.
3. Gather documents; see the [document checklist](/insights/investor-pass-document-checklist).
4. File the certificate application, then move to residency.
