Most descriptions of the Paraguay Investor Pass are summaries of summaries. This one is taken from the resolution itself. It is written for investors who want to know exactly what they are signing up to, and for migration agents and family offices who need to advise on it. Where the text is silent, we say so.

## The main text

The resolution itself is short, eight articles. **Article 1** adopts the requirements in Annex I as the step before permanent residency is granted by the migration office. Its legal basis, cited in the preamble, is article 46 of the migration law, which exempts from temporary residency foreigners who reliably demonstrate investment in Paraguay under the SUACE law.

**Article 2** puts the review with SUACE's Directorate of Investments and Special Regimes, which must control the documents exhaustively, evaluate the business plan and issue a technical report, "without prejudice" to the powers of SEPRELAD, Paraguay's anti-money-laundering secretariat. **Article 3** lets SUACE reject incomplete or non-compliant files. **Article 4** sets the issuing term, which stops running while SUACE's observations are answered.

**Article 5** is the one investors tend to overlook. It gives SUACE's verification directorate an ongoing role: following up on the requirements, keeping a statistical register of certificates, coordinating verifications against the business plan and sending the migration office reports on the certificates issued. **Articles 6 to 8** handle the transition, the repeal of the previous resolution and publication.

## Annex I: definitions and the four routes

The annex defines the certificate and the investor, then the four routes:

- **Productive investments**: industry, commerce or services, under a previously approved business plan, with job creation. <Fact k="investorpass.route_business_usd" />.
- **Financial instruments**: capital contributed to the country, with no obligation to manage a business or create jobs. <Fact k="investorpass.route_financial_usd" />.
- **Real estate**: acquisition, development or exploitation of property or recognized real rights, to earn rent, appreciation or productive use, excluding purchases for exclusively personal or family use. <Fact k="investorpass.route_real_estate_usd" />.
- **Tourism**: creating, buying, expanding, modernizing or operating tourism assets, infrastructure or services, under a previously approved business plan. <Fact k="investorpass.route_tourism_usd" />.

It also settles two points that often cause confusion: <Fact k="investorpass.investment_status" />. "Formal employment" is defined simply as employment that complies with labor law.

## What the resolution does not say

Advisers should be clear about the gaps, because clients assume the text covers more than it does.

- **Family members.** The certificate is issued to individual investors only. Spouses and children are dealt with by the migration office under migration rules; see our [family guide](/insights/bringing-your-family).
- **The residency decision.** The resolution sets no term for the migration office's stage and no residency fee. Our working estimate for the whole process is <Fact k="investorpass.timeline" />.
- **Selling or winding down.** The text sets reporting and verification but no explicit clause on what happens to residency if the investment is sold or the project abandoned. Treat that as an open question to settle before investing, not as permission.
- **Tax.** Nothing in the resolution changes how the investor is taxed. See [the Investor Pass and your taxes](/insights/investor-pass-and-your-taxes).

## Notes for agents and family offices

- Co-investors in one project must each meet the minimum individually. The annex says so in the productive-route article, in words that refer to every type of investment it defines.
- If the investor is not named in a company's statutes, the file needs the share-transfer notice or the beneficial-ownership record from the Ministry of Economy and Finance's registry.
- Source-of-funds evidence is reviewed with SEPRELAD's powers expressly reserved. Structure the paper trail before the transfer, not after.
- Documents for the financial and real estate routes must be recent when the file is filed, so time their issue with the filing date.
