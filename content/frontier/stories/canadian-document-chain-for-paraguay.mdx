Your Canadian documents may be scattered across the country without feeling especially international. A birth registered in one province, a marriage recorded elsewhere, a police check arranged near your current home: ordinary life produces that folder. It becomes a different kind of problem when the folder needs to support a Paraguay residency application.

The passport says Canada. The authentication route needs more detail. Which authority issued each record? What kind of police check do you hold? Is the document an official certificate or a copy someone has notarised? Answering those questions is how you avoid sending a plausible-looking package along the wrong path.

## Start with a map of the records

Make a list of the civil documents your case needs and put the issuing province, territory or federal authority beside each. Record the actual issuer rather than the location where you happen to keep the paper. Your present address does not relocate a birth registration or change the signature on a marriage certificate.

Have the available certificate format checked before requesting a replacement. A civil registry may offer records with different levels of detail. The shortest option is not automatically enough for Paraguay, and the most elaborate option is not automatically necessary. Ask what information the filing team needs to establish and which official record provides it.

If you were born outside Canada, add that country to the map. Citizenship and document origin are separate questions. A foreign birth certificate does not acquire a Canadian apostille route merely because you became Canadian or had a copy made there. Ask how the original public record needs to be authenticated for your application.

## Canada has an apostille route, with divided responsibility

Canada and Paraguay are members of the Hague Apostille Convention. For covered public documents, an apostille replaces the consular legalisation chain. Older advice about an authentication stamp followed by consular legalisation should therefore be checked before you act on it.

Canada's federal and provincial arrangements mean the competent authority is not necessarily the same for every document. The route can depend on the document type and where it was issued or notarised. Some records follow a provincial route and others the designated federal route. Confirm the current allocation instead of assuming everything goes through a national office.

The [Canadian authentication requirements](https://international.canada.ca/en/global-affairs/services/document-authentication/requirements) are a useful starting point for identifying the applicable process. Use the original document's details when checking it. If a provider proposes notarising a copy, ask whether that changes the competent authority and whether the resulting document is suitable for Paraguay.

An apostille confirms the relevant signature or seal. It does not settle whether the receiving office accepts a particular certificate format. Resolving the apostille destination is necessary, but it is not the whole document review. You still need to know that the underlying evidence belongs in your residency file.

## Be precise about the criminal-record product

For a Canadian applicant, the likely starting point to discuss is a certified national criminal-record check through the national police records authority. Fingerprints may be central to that product, with collection or submission handled through an appropriate local service. Confirm the exact category and purpose required for your Paraguay route before arranging it.

The official result identifies the applicant and reports the outcome of the relevant records enquiry. Local police services can also offer checks for particular purposes. A check obtained for work or volunteering should not be treated as interchangeable with the national record your filing team requests. Similar names on application menus can conceal a meaningful difference in coverage or disclosure.

The [official Canadian criminal-record guidance](https://rcmp.ca/en/criminal-records/criminal-record-checks) distinguishes the certified fingerprint-based process and the arrangements for applicants inside or outside Canada. Check your circumstances against the current instructions. The company taking fingerprints may facilitate the request without being the issuer of the resulting certificate.

Ask where the final document will be delivered and what form can enter the authentication chain. A submission receipt is evidence that you applied, not the clearance itself. If you are already abroad, resolve the accepted collection and forwarding arrangements before assuming a local fingerprint appointment completes the Canadian part of the work.

## English and French both meet the local translation rule

Use the same rule applied in our residency documents guidance: Paraguay filing translations need a certified or sworn translator recognised locally in Paraguay. A Canadian translator's certification is not sufficient by itself. Neither an English original nor a bilingual English and French certificate removes the need to check the local translation requirement.

Normally, authenticate the records before arranging their filing translations. Give the translator the complete civil certificate, criminal-record response, apostille and relevant endorsements. If part of the document is in French and another part in English, flag that when arranging the work so the complete package is reviewed.

Check accents, names and any change of surname against the passport. Differences can have an explanation without being something the translator is free to erase. Identify the supporting evidence or ask the issuer about a correction where necessary. Translation should represent the record faithfully, including the details that deserve attention.

## Know when the Canadian file is ready to leave your hands

Keep reference scans and track who holds each original. Before forwarding a package, check that the expected certificate actually arrived and that the authentication belongs with it. If responsibility passes to a service provider, make sure document review is explicitly included rather than assuming collection includes acceptance checks.
