A laptop job and a Plan B are a natural pairing: your income does not depend on where you sleep. Paraguay fits, but not because of a special visa. This page explains what does work, what does not and where the American tax system still reaches.

## The visa

As far as we know there is no digital-nomad visa. You apply through the ordinary residency route, so start at [our routes](/routes). What makes it workable for a remote worker is proving means.

## Showing your income

There is no fixed amount. Means are shown by category: <Fact k="solvency.requirement" />. Typical evidence:

- **W-2 employees:** an employer letter and pay statements.
- **1099 contractors:** contracts, invoices and bank statements.
- **Business owners:** company records and what you draw.

US documents usually need an apostille and translation. The chain is laid out in [the US document chain](/stories/us-document-chain-before-paraguay).

## Tax: Paraguay

Paraguay's side: <Fact k="tax.foreign_income_treatment" />. That is what draws remote workers. It depends on where the work is really performed and on how it is structured; it is not automatic.

## Tax: the United States

The US taxes citizens on worldwide income wherever they live, and <Fact k="tax.double_tax_treaties" />. Residency in Paraguay does not change your US filing. Exclusions have their own tests. Read [US taxes while holding Paraguay residency](/stories/us-taxes-while-holding-paraguay-residency), then talk to a US-qualified tax professional. We are not one.

## Travel
