Health insurance is a practical question every new resident eventually has to
answer, and the honest picture is: private insurance in Paraguay is
accessible, generally good in Asunción, and worth understanding before you
need it rather than after.

## Public vs. private

Paraguay has a public healthcare system, but the realistic pattern among
foreign residents is to use private insurance and private clinics, mainly for
faster appointment access, generally shorter wait times, and — depending on
the provider — English-speaking staff, which matters a great deal in a
medical setting if your Spanish isn't yet fluent.

## What private plans typically cover

Plans vary by provider and tier, but generally include outpatient
consultations, hospitalization, and a range of diagnostic services. As with
any insurance market, the details that actually matter — pre-existing
condition handling, specialist referral rules, and network hospital lists —
differ enough between providers that comparing at least two or three plans
directly is worth the time before committing to one.

## What to check before choosing a plan

- **Network hospitals and clinics**, especially if you plan to live outside
  Asunción, where private-network coverage is thinner.
- **Pre-existing condition treatment**, which is handled very differently
  provider to provider and can materially affect what you're actually
  covered for.
- **Emergency and evacuation coverage**, particularly relevant if you're
  further from Asunción's larger hospitals.
- **Cost**, which varies by age, coverage tier, and provider — get a current
  quote directly rather than relying on a general figure, since pricing
  changes and depends heavily on your own age and health profile. [VERIFY
  current premium ranges directly with providers for your age group before
  budgeting.]
