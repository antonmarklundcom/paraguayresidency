The cédula is your Paraguayan identity document and, practically, the thing that unlocks day-to-day life as a resident — full banking, formal employment, and most administrative processes expect it. Here is what the collection process, timeline and cost actually look like, and where a power of attorney fits in.

## How long does it take to get a cédula after approval?

{{fact:cedula.timeline}}. In practice it can run longer during high-volume periods and because of enhanced Interpol verification with the country of origin introduced in 2026 — don't schedule anything (a lease, a job start date) that depends on having it by an exact date.

## Can a power of attorney replace being there in person?

A power of attorney is a legal authorization letting a representative act on your behalf — historically used so someone else can handle parts of the cédula process without the applicant present for every appointment. What it typically cannot replace is the biometric capture step (photo and fingerprints), which generally requires your physical presence at least once. Confirm the current split between what a representative can do and what requires you personally before planning your travel around it — administrative practice here changes without much public notice.

## Why this matters more than it might seem

Getting the collection logistics wrong — assuming you can handle everything remotely when current practice requires an in-person step, or the reverse — means an extra, avoidable trip. Since the cédula is the document that unlocks full banking (see the [banking article](/blog/opening-a-bank-account-in-paraguay)), a delay here cascades into everything downstream.

## What to confirm before you plan around it

- Which specific steps require your physical presence versus what a power of attorney can cover for your case.
- The current cédula timeline for your situation — {{fact:cedula.timeline}} is typical, but ask your case handler for a current estimate.
- Whether any recent procedural change affects your specific residency category — this is exactly the kind of detail that updates without wide notice.

## The pattern worth remembering

Administrative procedures around identity documents change without the fanfare of a headline immigration-law update. Build in a habit of confirming procedural specifics — collection process, required presence, documents to bring — close to when you'll actually need them.
