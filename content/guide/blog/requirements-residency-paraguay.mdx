Paraguay offers a few distinct routes to residency, and the single most
common early mistake is picking one before actually comparing them against
your own situation. Here's the plain overview.

## Temporary residency

The most common starting point for new applicants. It generally requires a
core document set (identity documents, a clean background check, proof of
means of support) and leads toward permanent residency after
<Fact k="temporary.duration" />. Full document specifics are covered in
[documents you need for Paraguay residency](/blog/documents-you-need-for-paraguay-residency).

## Permanent residency

Permanent status is the longer-term goal for most applicants, whether reached
directly or after a period on temporary status. It carries an ongoing
obligation worth understanding up front: <Fact k="permanent.presence_rule" />.
This is the rule people misquote most often in this niche, so read it
carefully rather than assuming.

## Residency by investment

A separate route for applicants making a qualifying investment in Paraguay,
generally with a faster or more direct path to permanent status in exchange
for the investment commitment. Investment thresholds and qualifying
categories are set by current government policy and change — see our sister
site for investor-specific detail, and always confirm the current qualifying
amount and category directly rather than relying on a fixed figure from any
single source. [VERIFY current investment thresholds and qualifying
categories before treating this route as a plan.]

## Which route actually fits you

- If you want the lowest-friction entry point and are comfortable with a
  standard process, temporary residency leading to permanent is the common
  path.
- If you're making a substantial investment in Paraguay anyway (business,
  real estate, financial instruments), the investment route may be worth
  comparing directly against the standard path for time saved.
- If your timeline matters more than anything else, read
  [how long the process actually takes](/blog/how-long-paraguay-residency-actually-takes)
  before committing to a route based on assumptions about speed.

## Tax residency is a separate question

Getting legal residency and becoming a tax resident are related but distinct.
Paraguay's tax system is territorial: <Fact k="tax.territorial_rate" />. That
is a starting point for a conversation with your own accountant, not a
substitute for one.

This overview is informational, not legal advice. Requirements change, and a
qualified immigration professional should confirm the specifics for your
nationality and situation before you file anything.
