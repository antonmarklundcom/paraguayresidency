Paraguay is not for everyone. It is unusually good for people who want a second residency with a low ongoing burden, and a poor fit for people who want a place they'll move to next month without visiting first.

## What should Paraguay residency actually do for you?

The useful question is what changes in your life if the application succeeds. Maybe you want somewhere you've actually visited and could make home. Maybe you want a second base while keeping work and family elsewhere. Those are concrete reasons to investigate residency. Collecting a card because an advertisement calls it easy is a weaker one — and if you can't explain the benefit without mentioning someone else's tax savings, pause before paying for an application.

## Who does Paraguay residency actually suit?

| Fits well | Fits poorly |
|---|---|
| Wants a genuine second residency held in reserve | Wants an outcome guaranteed in advance |
| Comfortable preparing documents before travel | Needs approval to coincide exactly with quitting a job |
| Has visited, or plans a real scouting trip | A partner unconvinced about living there |
| Budget covers the total, not just the advertised price | Main expectation is an automatic tax change or a bank account |

For someone considering a genuine move, a scouting visit answers questions an immigration consultation can't — test the neighborhood, the commute, the services your household uses. For someone seeking a backup base, the test is different: would you actually return, keep your records organised, and follow the obligations attached to your status?

## What does Paraguay actually do to my taxes?

Paraguay uses a territorial system: <Fact k="tax.foreign_income_treatment" />. That is a starting point for a conversation with your own accountant, not advice, and it is never "tax-free." Bring that conversation forward if tax is central to your decision — explain where you work, where your clients are, and what connections you keep to your current country. Try making the decision without assuming a tax saving; if residency still looks worthwhile, you have a reason independent of an unresolved calculation.

Residency is worth pursuing when its purpose is clear, the <Fact k="costs.diy_total" /> total budget is comfortable, and the presence obligations fit your travel pattern.
