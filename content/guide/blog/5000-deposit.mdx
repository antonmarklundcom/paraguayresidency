If you've seen older content online mentioning a specific bank-deposit figure
as a Paraguay residency requirement, treat it as outdated rather than
current. Requirements like this are set by government policy and get
revised — a figure that was accurate when a page was written is not
guaranteed to still be accurate by the time you read it. [VERIFY the current
means-of-support requirement — whether a bank deposit, income proof, or
another form of demonstrated support — directly with a current official
source or immigration professional before assuming any specific figure from
online content, including this page.]

## Why this matters more than it might seem

A change like this affects how much you budget and prepare before applying,
and it's exactly the kind of detail where an outdated blog post can send
someone into the process with the wrong expectations — either overestimating
what they need to set aside, or underpreparing because they read a rule that
no longer applies.

## What to actually do

- Don't budget around a specific number you found in an old article — treat
  any such figure as a starting orientation, not a confirmed requirement.
- Confirm the current means-of-support requirement for your specific
  residency category, since requirements can differ between temporary,
  permanent, and investment-based routes — see our [requirements
  overview](/blog/requirements-residency-paraguay) for the route comparison.
- If a bank deposit or proof-of-funds requirement does currently apply to
  your route, get the current figure and format requirements in writing from
  a current, dated source before you travel.

## The broader pattern

Paraguay's residency rules, like most countries', are periodically updated.
Treat any number-specific claim about fees, deposits, or thresholds you find
online — including elsewhere on this site — as something to verify against a
current source rather than something to plan a move around unconfirmed.
