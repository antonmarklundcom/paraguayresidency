Getting connected is one of the first small logistics tasks after landing, and it's genuinely simple compared to the residency process itself — but a few practical details save a wasted trip to a carrier store.

## What do I need to buy a SIM card in Paraguay?

- **Your passport**, generally accepted for a basic prepaid registration at a carrier store or authorized kiosk.
- **Cash or a card** for the initial SIM and top-up purchase — prices are modest by international standards, though exact current pricing is worth checking in-store rather than an old figure online.
- **Patience for registration**, which can involve more in-store paperwork than a similar purchase elsewhere. This is standard local practice, not a sign of anything unusual.

## Which carriers do new residents actually use?

Paraguay's mobile market is served by a small number of national carriers offering prepaid and postpaid plans. Prepaid is by far the more common starting choice for new arrivals, since it requires no local credit history or long-term commitment while you're still settling in.

## Where's the easiest place to buy one?

Carrier stores and kiosks are common in shopping centers and central commercial areas in Asunción and other major cities, including at the international airport — the easiest option if you want to be connected the moment you land.

## Can I get a SIM before my residency is approved?

Generally yes. A passport is typically enough for a basic prepaid line, and you don't need to wait for residency approval, a cédula, or anything else — this is one of the few logistics steps that doesn't depend on where you are in the residency process.

## What's the one thing that trips people up later?

Keep your SIM active with periodic top-ups even during trips home — a long-dormant prepaid line can be deactivated by the carrier, and reactivating or replacing it is more hassle than a small top-up every so often. This matters more if you're maintaining a Paraguayan number as a stable contact point through the residency process, since banks and other institutions may use it to reach you.
