Most nationalities researching Paraguay residency don't need to secure a visa
before flying in — they get a visa-exempt entry stamp on arrival and start the
residency process from there. A smaller list of nationalities needs to obtain
an entry visa in advance from a Paraguayan consulate. Which list you're on
depends entirely on your passport, and that list is set by the government, not
by us, so it's worth confirming directly before you book anything. [VERIFY —
check the current visa-exemption list for your specific nationality before
travel; it is periodically revised.]

## The two entry situations

**Visa-exempt.** You arrive, get a standard entry stamp, and begin the
residency filing process locally — no advance paperwork with a consulate.

**Visa-required.** You apply for an entry visa at a Paraguayan consulate in
your home country (or the nearest one that handles your nationality) before
you travel. This adds lead time to your trip planning, so it's worth checking
early, not the week before you fly.

Either way, the entry visa (or its absence) is separate from the residency
application itself — it only governs how you get into the country. Filing for
residency is a distinct step in person, [with its own document
list](/blog/documents-you-need-for-paraguay-residency).

## Where people get this wrong

The most common mistake is confusing "I don't need a visa to enter" with "I
don't need to formally file for residency." They're unrelated. A tourist
stamp lets you visit; it does not grant residency status on its own, and it
has its own limited validity window. If your plan is to live in Paraguay
rather than visit, the residency filing is the step that actually matters —
see [how long that process actually takes](/blog/how-long-paraguay-residency-actually-takes).

## Before you book a flight

- Confirm your nationality's current entry requirement directly (consulate
  website or a recent, dated source) — don't rely on an old blog post,
  including this one, for that specific fact.
- If you need an entry visa, start that process well before your intended
  travel date; consulate processing times vary and are outside anyone's
  direct control.
- Bring the documents you'll need for the residency filing itself with you —
  arriving on the right kind of stamp doesn't help if your paperwork isn't
  ready.

This page is informational and not a substitute for checking current entry
requirements with a Paraguayan consulate or qualified immigration counsel for
your specific nationality.
