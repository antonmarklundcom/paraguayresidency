Paraguay shows up on relocation lists for three real reasons: a low, publicly stated bar to
get permanent residency, a <Fact k="tax.territorial_rate" /> tax system, and a genuinely low
cost of living outside a handful of imported goods. None of that makes it a good fit for
everyone, and this chapter is where we say so before you spend a weekend reading the rest.

**It suits you if:** you want a second residency or tax base without years of waiting, you can
work remotely or your income does not depend on being physically present somewhere else, and
you are comfortable with a country that is still building out the conveniences a European or
North American city takes for granted.

**It does not suit you if:** you need advanced medical care outside Asunción on short notice,
you expect government offices to move at the pace of a private company, or your only reason for
moving is a headline "zero tax" claim — Paraguay is not that, and anyone who tells you otherwise
is selling something.

**The honest trade-off:** residency here is comparatively fast and comparatively cheap to
obtain. What you get in exchange is a smaller, less internationally connected country. Chapter 2
compares the three routes into that trade-off in detail; chapter 4 puts real numbers on it.
