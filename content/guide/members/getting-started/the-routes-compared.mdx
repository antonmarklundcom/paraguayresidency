Three routes lead to the same card. Which one is right for you depends on timeline, budget and
how sure you already are.

**Temporary residency** is the standard first step for most applicants. <Fact k="temporary.duration" />
It is the cheapest and least document-heavy route, and it converts into permanent residency
later without starting over.

**Permanent residency** is the ten-year card most people actually want. Applying directly is
possible for some nationalities and case types; for others, temporary residency first is the
realistic path. <Fact k="permanent.presence_rule" /> — ask us about your specific case before you
assume either way.

**The Investor Pass** skips temporary residency entirely for a qualifying investment.
<Fact k="investorpass.min_investment_usd" /> across four routes (real estate, a productive
business, financial instruments, or tourism-sector investment), each with its own threshold —
our sibling brand, Paraguay Investor Pass, covers this route on its own.

**A simple way to choose:** if your timeline is flexible and your budget is modest, temporary
residency is the default. If you want permanent residency in one filing and have qualifying
capital, ask us about the Investor Pass before you commit to anything else — it can be
materially faster even after its own processing time.
