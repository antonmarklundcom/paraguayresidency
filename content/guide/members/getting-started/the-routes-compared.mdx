Choosing a route is a decision about eligibility, commitments and what happens after approval. It is not simply a race to the word "permanent". We compare temporary residency, a permanent-residency application where available to your case, and the Investor Pass because each asks something different of you.

Start with chapter 1, Why Paraguay (and why not), if you have not decided what you want residency to achieve. Here, your task is to narrow the route and identify what must be confirmed before you spend money collecting documents or arranging travel.

## Temporary residency: plan the next step now

<Fact k="temporary.duration" /> The important word is "apply": do not treat a later permanent application as an automatic conversion that takes care of itself. Ask what you would need to retain, update and submit when that stage arrives.

**It suits you if:** you want to establish a base without making an investment solely for immigration purposes, and you can manage a later application. You still need a credible document and travel plan. Temporary does not mean informal, and a simpler personal situation does not remove the need to check eligibility.

**It does not suit you if:** your plan depends on receiving permanent status immediately and there is a concrete reason that temporary status will not meet your needs. Explain that reason. Sometimes the supposed requirement is an assumption about banking or tax that needs checking with the institution concerned.

Before choosing this path, ask for a written outline of the initial filing and the later transition. Keep the future step in your budget and calendar even though you are not doing it yet.

## Permanent residency: establish the basis

The [Migraciones residence directory](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencias/) distinguishes residence categories. Your question is which category fits your circumstances and what prior status or other evidence it requires. A label on a provider's website is not an eligibility assessment.

Tell us about your citizenships, existing Paraguayan status and relevant family circumstances. Ask the adviser to name the applicable category and explain why you qualify. If the answer is that temporary residency comes first, make sure the proposed service describes that sequence clearly.

**It suits you if:** there is a confirmed legal basis for the permanent application you are proposing. **It does not suit you if:** the only evidence is that someone with a different nationality or immigration history obtained it directly.

After approval, maintenance still matters. <Fact k="permanent.presence_rule" /> Confirm your obligations against your expected travel pattern. Status, the document proving it and the administrative steps needed to keep that document usable should be discussed separately.

## Nationality can change the comparison

Do not force every passport into the same route chart. <Fact k="mercosur.residency_route" /> Ask which route is being proposed for your nationality and what that changes in practice: eligibility, documents, sequencing or a later application.

If you hold more than one citizenship, disclose that before a checklist is prepared. Also explain where your civil records were issued and where you have lived. The passport used for a route does not magically relocate your birth certificate or erase a residence history.

Chapter 3, Documents, apostilles and translations, by nationality, owns that document work. The decision here is to obtain a route-specific list rather than collect a generic bundle and hope it fits.

## Investor Pass: capital adds another decision

The Investor Pass is the investment-linked option to assess if direct permanent residency is your objective. The threshold belongs in the facts registry: <Fact k="investorpass.min_investment_usd" />. Confirm current eligibility and qualifying conditions before selecting an asset or transferring funds.

**It suits you if:** you already have a defensible reason to invest in Paraguay and the proposed investment can be checked for programme eligibility. **It does not suit you if:** you need the capital readily available or would accept a poor investment only because it is packaged with residency.

Avoid treating an investment amount as a service fee. You need to understand what you own, who controls it, what could reduce its value and how you could eventually exit. Chapter 10, Investor Pass overview, gives you the screening questions without turning this comparison into an investment prospectus.

## Compare the complete paths

Create a route note with these headings: eligibility evidence, documents outstanding, personal attendance, money committed, later applications and unresolved questions. Complete it for each realistic option. Leave an unknown marked as unknown; do not let an optimistic assumption become a deciding advantage.

Compare the proposed scope with chapter 4, Costs, real ones. A quote ending at initial submission is different from support through approval, identity documents and a later application. Chapter 5, Timeline, week by week, explains how to compare dependencies instead of advertised finish dates.

Ask what would make the recommendation change. A missing civil record, a household member needing a separate route or an investment that does not qualify can alter the comparison. A useful adviser can explain those turning points.

**Your decision:** choose a provisional route and list the evidence required to confirm it. Do not book around the fastest headline. Proceed when you understand why you qualify, what you must do next and which future obligations you are accepting.
