The Investor Pass is covered in full depth on its own brand, Paraguay Investor Pass — this
chapter is the short version: what it is, and when it earns its cost over the standard route.

**What it is:** direct permanent residency for a qualifying investment, skipping temporary
residency entirely. <Fact k="investorpass.min_investment_usd" /> across four routes: real estate
<Fact k="investorpass.route_real_estate_usd" />, a productive business
<Fact k="investorpass.route_business_usd" />, financial instruments
<Fact k="investorpass.route_financial_usd" />, or a tourism-sector investment
<Fact k="investorpass.route_tourism_usd" />.

**When it is worth it:** if you already plan to invest in Paraguay — property, a business, or
capital you were going to place somewhere — routing that investment through the Investor Pass
gets you permanent residency as a side effect of a decision you were making anyway. It is a
different calculation if you would only invest in order to get the card; then you are comparing
its cost against the time saved versus the standard route, and that comparison is worth doing on
a call rather than assuming either way.

**Where to go deeper:** paraguayinvestorpass.com covers requirements, the four routes and the
application process in full.
