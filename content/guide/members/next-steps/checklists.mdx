This chapter is the one to print. Each list is short enough to fit on a page and specific enough to tick with confidence. A tick means the evidence is in your folder — not that you've read about it. If an item doesn't apply, write "n/a" and who confirmed it.

The earlier chapters explain the why. Here is the what.

## 1. Deciding (chapters 1 and 2)

- [ ] I have written my reason to proceed, my strongest reason to pause, and the missing evidence
- [ ] I know which route I am using: temporary → permanent, Mercosur, or Investor Pass
- [ ] I have checked my travel plans against the presence rule: <Fact k="temporary.presence_rule" />
- [ ] I know which proof-of-means category I expect to use at conversion under Res. 407/2026
- [ ] I have listed every country I have lived in during the police-certificate period
- [ ] Anyone with a criminal record entry has discussed it before paying for documents
- [ ] I have booked a home-country tax adviser (chapter 8)

## 2. Documents from home — everyone (chapter 3)

- [ ] Passport valid for the whole process and my travel
- [ ] Full (long-form) birth certificate naming both parents
- [ ] Marriage certificate, if married
- [ ] Name-change documents for every difference between documents
- [ ] National police certificate, timed to be <Fact k="documents.police_certificate_validity" />
- [ ] Police certificates from every other country lived in during the period
- [ ] Every foreign document apostilled (Paraguay is <Fact k="apostille.since" />) or legalised
- [ ] Names checked letter by letter across passport and every certificate
- [ ] Complete scans reviewed before booking flights

## 3. By nationality group

**United States**

- [ ] FBI Identity History Summary (fingerprint-based) — not a state or local check
- [ ] FBI report apostilled by the US Department of State
- [ ] Birth and marriage records apostilled by the Secretary of State of the **issuing** state
- [ ] Bank asked whether it accepts US persons (chapter 7)

**United Kingdom**

- [ ] ACRO Police Certificate
- [ ] Full birth certificate, not the short version
- [ ] FCDO apostilles; checked whether any document needs solicitor or notary certification first

**Canada**

- [ ] RCMP certified (fingerprint-based) criminal record check
- [ ] Federal documents to Global Affairs Canada; provincial documents to the provincial authority where one exists
- [ ] Long-form provincial birth certificate

**Australia**

- [ ] AFP National Police Check, for overseas or immigration use
- [ ] Full birth certificate from the state or territory registry
- [ ] DFAT apostilles

**Germany and EU**

- [ ] National criminal record extract (Führungszeugnis for Germany) for use abroad
- [ ] Full civil records, not multilingual EU forms alone
- [ ] Apostille from the competent authority for each document

**Spain**

- [ ] Certificado de Antecedentes Penales, apostilled
- [ ] Certificado literal de nacimiento, apostilled
- [ ] No translation needed — documents already in Spanish

**South Africa**

- [ ] SAPS Police Clearance Certificate (fingerprint-based), applied for early enough to arrive inside the window
- [ ] Unabridged birth certificate
- [ ] DIRCO apostilles; confirmed which office signs first

**LatAm and Mercosur**

- [ ] Confirmed eligibility: <Fact k="mercosur.residency_route" />
- [ ] National criminal record certificate, apostilled
- [ ] Portuguese-language documents (Brazil) translated in Paraguay
- [ ] Confirmed what counts as proof of legal entry if entering on a national ID card

## 4. Before the flight to Asunción

- [ ] Scans sent to a Paraguayan registered public translator; names checked against passport
- [ ] Accommodation near the offices, for the whole working week
- [ ] Cost worksheet filled in (chapter 4) with a contingency line
- [ ] Guaraníes plan for cash-only fees
- [ ] All originals in one folder, copies in another
- [ ] Return flight flexible, or a buffer day built in

## 5. What to bring to each appointment

**Interpol certificate**

- [ ] Passport, original and copy
- [ ] Cash: <Fact k="fees.interpol_certificate" />
- [ ] Time for fingerprinting and a possible second visit to collect

**Paraguayan police certificate**

- [ ] Passport, original and copy
- [ ] Fee: <Fact k="fees.police_certificate_py" />

**Translator**

- [ ] Originals to compare against the translations
- [ ] Passport, for name spelling
- [ ] Payment

**DNM filing**

- [ ] Passport, original and copies
- [ ] Apostilled civil documents with sworn translations
- [ ] National police certificate(s), apostilled and translated, still inside the window
- [ ] Paraguayan police and Interpol certificates
- [ ] Completed DNM forms and sworn declaration
- [ ] Paraguayan address
- [ ] Fee: <Fact k="fees.temporary_residency" /> (per person)
- [ ] Leave with the filing receipt

**Identificaciones (cédula)**

- [ ] Passport, resident card and DNM resolution
- [ ] Apostilled, translated birth (and marriage) certificates
- [ ] Current Paraguayan police and Interpol certificates — fresh if the old ones are stale
- [ ] Paraguayan address and phone number
- [ ] Checked every letter of the form before signing
- [ ] Fee: <Fact k="fees.cedula_first" />

**DNIT (RUC)**

- [ ] Cédula
- [ ] Proof of address
- [ ] Email and Paraguayan phone number
- [ ] Activity agreed with an accountant

**Bank**

- [ ] Cédula and passport
- [ ] Proof of address
- [ ] Income evidence covering recent months
- [ ] Source-of-funds documents for planned large transfers
- [ ] RUC, if the bank asks
- [ ] Tax self-certification details (all tax residences and numbers)

## 6. After approval (chapters 6 and 7)

- [ ] Trip 2 booked only after confirming the resident card is ready
- [ ] Cédula applied for promptly
- [ ] Cédula received and data checked
- [ ] RUC decision made with an accountant
- [ ] Bank account opened and tested with a small transfer
- [ ] Annual entry reminder in the calendar
- [ ] Income-evidence folder started for conversion

## 7. Converting to permanent

- [ ] Conversion window in the calendar: temporary residency is <Fact k="temporary.duration" />
- [ ] Category chosen under Res. 407/2026: <Fact k="solvency.requirement" />
- [ ] Income or asset evidence: consistent names, sources and amounts across contracts, statements and tax records
- [ ] Foreign evidence apostilled and translated
- [ ] RUC and filing history, if relying on it
- [ ] Fee: <Fact k="fees.permanent_residency" /> (per person)
- [ ] After approval: presence rule noted — <Fact k="permanent.presence_rule" />

## 8. Family (chapter 9)

- [ ] Name grid: every person, every document, every spelling matching
- [ ] Marriage certificate apostilled and translated
- [ ] Each child's full birth certificate naming both parents
- [ ] Consent or custody order for any child whose other parent is not moving
- [ ] Police certificates for every adult ordered together, all inside the window on filing day
- [ ] Main applicant's income evidence covers the whole household at conversion
- [ ] School records apostilled, translated and recognition started
- [ ] Travel authorisation for minors carried on every trip

## 9. Investor Pass (chapter 10)

- [ ] Route chosen, and the investment makes sense without the residency
- [ ] Qualification confirmed in writing before investing
- [ ] Structure (personal or company) reviewed for tax
- [ ] Source-of-funds file complete
- [ ] Investment executed and documented
- [ ] CIE obtained from MIC
- [ ] DNM permanent residency filed with personal documents
- [ ] Continuing conditions (jobs, holding period, property use) evidenced

## 10. Every year, from now on

- [ ] Entered Paraguay within the presence period
- [ ] DNM fees checked after the July update (<Fact k="fees.basis" />)
- [ ] Bank information requests answered
- [ ] Tax filings made, if registered for a RUC
- [ ] Home-country adviser consulted on any change
