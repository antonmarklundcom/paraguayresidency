Use this chapter as the working list while you go — everything on it is explained in an earlier
chapter, linked below.

**Before you travel**
- Valid passport with at least six months remaining
- Birth certificate, apostilled or legalised (chapter 3)
- Police clearance certificate(s), issued recently and apostilled or legalised (chapter 3)
- Marriage certificate, if applicable (chapter 9)
- Travel booked around a confirmed filing appointment (chapter 5)

**On arrival**
- Biometrics and initial filing (chapter 5)
- Certified local translations of any non-Spanish document (chapter 3)

**After approval**
- Collect the cédula de identidad (chapter 6)
- Register for the RUC (chapter 6)
- Open a local bank account (chapter 7)
- Confirm your tax position with an adviser if your situation is not simple employment income
  (chapter 8)

**Route-specific extras**
- Investor Pass: investment documentation for your chosen route (chapter 10)
- Family applications: each dependent's own document set (chapter 9)

This checklist is deliberately general — your exact document list depends on nationality and
route. The downloadable, printable version in Members → Resources tracks any updates to
requirements between guide editions.
