Every cost below is a range, not a quote — your exact figure depends on nationality, how many
documents need legalisation rather than a single apostille, and whether you file alone or with
help.

**Government fees:** the application, the cédula de identidad, and the RUC each carry their own
government fee, paid in guaraníes at the time of filing. These change with inflation more often
than most guides admit, which is why we don't print a fixed number here — you'll get the current
figure on a call, and it is a small fraction of the total cost either way.

**Document costs:** apostilles or legalisation, certified translations once you're in Paraguay,
and courier costs if documents travel between countries. Budget more if your country requires
consular legalisation rather than an apostille — it usually costs more and takes longer.

**Travel:** at least one trip to Paraguay is required for biometrics and in-person filing, plus
typically one further visit for the cédula pickup, though both can sometimes be combined into one
trip with the right timing.

**Professional fees:** if you use a service like ours, the fee is fixed and quoted before you
commit — no hourly billing, no surprise add-ons partway through. Chapter 12's checklist is
usable start to finish without paying anyone if you would rather self-file; most readers use it
alongside professional help rather than instead of it.
