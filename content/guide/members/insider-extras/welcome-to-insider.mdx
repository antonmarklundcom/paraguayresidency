The twelve chapters you already have are the complete guide — Insider does not gate any of that
behind a paywall you have already paid past. What it adds is everything that changes *after*
publication.

**What you get every month:** a short update covering anything that moved since last time —
a fee change, a processing-time shift, a new document requirement, a policy clarification we
picked up from a filing. Paraguay residency rules move; a static PDF cannot keep up with that on
its own, and this is the part that does.

**Deeper material than the guide covers:** case studies from real filings (anonymised), the
edge cases that do not fit a general-audience guide, and early notes on anything that looks like
it is about to change before it is confirmed enough to publish widely.

**How the drip works here:** this welcome lesson is open the moment you join. The next deep dive
opens a little after that, on the same monthly rhythm the membership is built around — check
Members → Updates for the running list, and this dashboard for what unlocks next.
