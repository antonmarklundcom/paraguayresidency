Paraguay residency applications generally scale by household rather than forcing each family
member through a separate, unrelated process — but each person still needs their own document
set.

**Spouses:** a spouse applies alongside the primary applicant using the same route, with a
marriage certificate added to their own core document set (chapter 3). The marriage certificate
needs the same apostille or legalisation treatment as everything else.

**Children:** minor children are typically included on a parent's application with a birth
certificate and, depending on the case, consent from both parents where only one is applying with
the child. Older dependents (adult children, parents) do not automatically qualify as dependents
and need their own route — ask us about your specific household before assuming everyone travels
together on paper as easily as they will in person.

**What does not change per family member:** the underlying route (temporary, permanent, or
Investor Pass) is the same for the whole household applying together; what multiplies is the
document collection and translation work, not the legal complexity of the case itself.
