Approval is not the finish line. Two more documents turn a residency approval into something you
can actually use day to day.

**The cédula de identidad** is Paraguay's national ID card. <Fact k="cedula.timeline" /> Once
issued, it is what you show for everything from opening a bank account to signing a lease — carry
it, not your passport, for local transactions.

**The RUC (Registro Único del Contribuyente)** is your tax identification number. You do not
need to be running a business to want one — it is also what a bank, a landlord or a utility
company will ask for once you are a resident, and it is the number your tax residency (chapter
8) is built on.

**A sequencing note:** the cédula and RUC are usually obtained in that order, and both require
the approval letter from your residency case first — there is no way to get either one before
that. Plan the timing of your second trip (if you need one) around when both are ready to
collect, rather than making two separate visits.
