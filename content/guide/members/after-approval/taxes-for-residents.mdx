<Fact k="tax.territorial_rate" /> That single sentence is the whole system in outline — the
detail is in what counts as Paraguayan-source income, and that is where a generalist guide like
this one should stop and a tax adviser should start.

**What this generally means for you:** income earned from work or business conducted outside
Paraguay is typically outside the Paraguayan tax net, while income sourced within Paraguay —
local employment, a local business, Paraguayan rental income — is taxed here. <Fact k="tax.foreign_income_treatment" />

**What it does not mean:** it is not a blanket "no taxes anywhere" outcome. Your home country may
still tax you depending on your citizenship, residency status there, and any tax treaty in force
— an exit from one country's tax system is a separate question from establishing residency in
Paraguay, and the two are often confused by people selling the second as if it solves the first.

**When to get a tax adviser:** before you assume anything about your specific structure — a
remote salary, a business you own, investment income, or a pension — behaves the way you expect.
This is the one chapter in the guide where "confirm with a professional before you act" is not a
hedge, it is the actual advice.
