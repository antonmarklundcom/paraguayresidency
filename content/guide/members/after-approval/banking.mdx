Opening a Paraguayan bank account is straightforward once you have a cédula — and difficult to
do meaningfully before you have one, so don't try to open an account on a tourist visit expecting
it to carry over.

**What you need:** your cédula, your RUC, proof of address (a utility bill or a signed rental
contract works), and — depending on the bank — a minimum opening deposit. Requirements vary more
between banks than between nationalities, so if one branch is slow or unhelpful, another often
is not.

**What to expect:** account opening itself is usually a single visit once your documents are in
order. Online banking and a debit card typically follow within a few business days. International
wire transfers in and out work, but expect more paperwork and slower processing than a transfer
between two accounts in the same country — factor that into how you plan to fund your first few
months here.

**A practical tip:** open your account as soon as your cédula is in hand, even if you do not need
it immediately. Rental deposits, utility connections and the RUC's ongoing tax filings all move
faster once you can point to a local account.
