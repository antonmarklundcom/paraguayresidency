Chapter 3 already tells you to keep your police certificate recent. This update is the kind of
on-the-ground detail that chapter does not have room for: how strictly that window is actually
enforced case by case, and which nationalities we have seen it matter most for recently.

If your certificate is close to the edge of the usual window, talk to us before you travel rather
than after — it is a much smaller fix in advance than it is once you are already in Asunción.
