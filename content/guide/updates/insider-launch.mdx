Paraguay Residency Insider is open. If you are reading this from inside Members, you already
have access — here is what that means in practice.

At launch, Insider includes the "Insider Extras" module (start with the welcome lesson), a
monthly deep dive going deeper than the twelve-chapter guide has room for, and this updates feed,
which is where every change — fee movements, processing-time shifts, new requirements — gets
posted as we see it, not batched into a once-a-year revision.

Nothing about your guide access changes. The twelve chapters remain yours, updated for as long as
your original 12 months of free updates run, exactly as promised at checkout.
