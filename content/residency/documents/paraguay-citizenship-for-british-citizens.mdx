Most British people who ask about Paraguayan citizenship are really asking two things: is a second passport realistic, and what does it cost in time and effort? This page answers the first honestly and points you to the steps in order. The short version: citizenship is the end of a long road, not a product you can buy, and residency is the only way in.

## The route in one line

Temporary residency, then permanent residency, then naturalisation. No step can be skipped. Our [residency services](/residency/permanent-residency) cover the first two; naturalisation is a separate court process that we help you prepare for but that needs a sponsoring lawyer.

## What the law asks

The minimum time is fixed: <Fact k="citizenship.years" />. The constitution sets the conditions: <Fact k="citizenship.constitutional_requirements" />. In practice the filing is judicial: <Fact k="citizenship.naturalization_requirements" />.

So there are three things to line up:

1. **Time.** The clock runs from the permanent residency resolution, not from your first arrival.
2. **A life in the country.** You need to show that you work, run a business or study there, and that your conduct is good.
3. **A lawyer.** The filing is made through one, with a legal address in Asunción.

The court fee is small next to legal fees: <Fact k="fees.naturalization_judicial" />.

## The dual nationality question

This is where British applicants need care. Paraguay's position is that <Fact k="citizenship.dual_nationality" />. And for someone who naturalises, <Fact k="citizenship.naturalized_loss" />. The UK side is generally permissive, but the Paraguayan side is what decides whether you can keep both. Do not build a plan on the assumption that you can. Ask for a written answer for your case first.

## What citizenship does and doesn't change

- It gives the nationality at once, and political rights later: <Fact k="citizenship.political_rights" />.
- It does not remove UK obligations. If you are thinking about tax, read [UK tax when moving to Paraguay](/guides/taxes/uk-tax-when-moving-to-paraguay) first.
- It does not replace good conduct: a residency built on a clean, honest file matters. If your record is an issue, see [residency with a criminal record](/guides/documents/paraguay-residency-with-a-criminal-record).

## Common mistakes

- Treating a residency card as a step towards a passport you can use in three years without checking the dual nationality rule.
- Leaving Paraguay for long periods and breaking the presence rules that keep residency alive.
- Waiting until year three to think about where your legal address and lawyer will be.

## A sensible order

1. Get the residency right first. Start with [what you need to apply](/guides/documents/what-you-need-to-apply).
2. Keep your presence and paperwork in order for the permanent stage.
3. Ask for a written view on dual nationality for your case before year three.
4. Instruct a lawyer for the court filing.
