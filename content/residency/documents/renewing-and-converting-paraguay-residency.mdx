The first residency card is the easy part to remember. The date that matters is the one printed on it. This page explains what happens next: converting to permanent, what an overstay costs, and the presence rules that keep either status alive. It applies to British applicants like everyone else; the only UK-specific point is that a UK document such as an ACRO certificate has a short shelf life and must be refreshed for the permanent stage.

## Temporary to permanent: the window

A temporary card lasts <Fact k="temporary.duration" />. The rule people miss is the timing: the change of category is possible <Fact k="permanent.change_window" />.

The fee for the permanent stage is <Fact k="fees.permanent_residency" />, and it is worth filing inside the window because the alternatives cost more.

## If you miss it

| Where you are | What it means | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Inside the window | The normal route | <Fact k="fees.permanent_residency" /> |
| Just after expiry | Still possible with a fine | Plus <Fact k="fees.overstay_fine" /> |
| Later | Only an extension of the temporary | <Fact k="fees.temporary_extension" /> and a fine |

These figures are set in minimum daily wages and change with the minimum wage, so confirm the current amount before paying. The whole budget is set out in [what Paraguay residency costs a UK applicant](/guides/documents/paraguay-residency-cost-for-uk-applicants).

## Renewing the permanent card

The residency has no end date, but the card does: <Fact k="fees.permanent_card_renewal" /> to renew every ten years. Put the date in your calendar when you collect it.

## Presence rules

Two rules run alongside the dates. For temporary residency, <Fact k="temporary.presence_rule" />. For permanent residency, <Fact k="permanent.presence_rule" />. Keep a simple log of entries and exits. If you are splitting time with the UK, the tax side matters too; see [UK tax when moving to Paraguay](/guides/taxes/uk-tax-when-moving-to-paraguay).

## What to prepare for the permanent stage

- A fresh police certificate if yours has aged out. For the UK, that means a new [ACRO certificate](/guides/documents/uk-police-certificate-acro-for-paraguay).
- Your proof of means, matched to your category.
- Your current card and the proof of your filing.

The general checklist is in [what you need to apply](/guides/documents/what-you-need-to-apply).

## Common mistakes

- Counting from the filing date rather than the card expiry.
- Ordering documents too early, so they expire before the appointment.
- Long absences that trip the presence rule.
- Assuming the permanent card never needs anything again.
