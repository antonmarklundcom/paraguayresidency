Most delays in a Paraguay residency file are not caused by the immigration
office. They are caused by a document that was issued too early, apostilled by
the wrong authority, or translated by a translator the office does not accept.

## The core set

Every applicant brings a birth certificate, a police clearance from the country
of residence, and proof of means. Each one has to be legalised for use in
Paraguay, and each one has its own clock.

## Why the order matters

Temporary residency runs for <Fact k="temporary.duration" />, so a document
collected too early can expire before the appointment it was collected for.

you which item to start first because it is the slowest.
