Remote work is the reason a lot of Britons look at Paraguay: a low cost of living, a residency that does not demand an investment and a tax system that focuses on local income. There are two traps. One is to expect a nomad visa that does not exist as far as we know. The other is to assume that moving abroad settles your UK tax. This page separates the two.

## The visa question

Remote workers, contractors and company owners apply for the ordinary [temporary residency](/residency/temporary-residency). There is no quicker or cheaper track because you work from a laptop. What makes it workable is how you show your means.

## Showing your income

There is no single fixed amount. Means are shown by category: <Fact k="solvency.requirement" />. Remote income can support an application if it is documented:

- **Employees:** contract or employer letter, plus statements showing regular pay.
- **Contractors:** client contracts, invoices and statements showing receipts.
- **Company owners:** company documents and evidence of what you draw.

UK documents usually need an apostille and translation; see [what you need to apply](/guides/documents/what-you-need-to-apply).

## Tax: Paraguay's side

Paraguay's side is simple: <Fact k="tax.foreign_income_treatment" />. That is why the country attracts remote workers. Two cautions: it depends on where the work is really done and how it is structured, and Paraguayan tax on local income is a separate matter, covered in [territorial tax explained](/guides/taxes/territorial-tax-explained).

## Tax: the UK side

This is the part to take advice on. Where you are tax resident is decided by the UK's Statutory Residence Test, not by where you live day to day, and <Fact k="uk.paraguay_tax_treaty" />. Read [UK tax when moving to Paraguay](/guides/taxes/uk-tax-when-moving-to-paraguay) first, then speak to a UK tax adviser. We are not one, and we would rather say so.

## Travelling while resident

Frequent travel is fine inside the rules. <Fact k="temporary.presence_rule" />; on permanent residency, <Fact k="permanent.presence_rule" />. Keep a log of entries and exits.

## Practical set-up

- **Internet:** test it before signing a long lease. Asunción tends to be steadier than smaller towns.
- **Money:** a local account helps day to day; see [opening a bank account](/guides/living-in-paraguay/opening-a-bank-account).
- **Timing:** ordering documents in the right sequence matters more than speed.
